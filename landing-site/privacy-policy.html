1. Introduction

Bombardment is a CLI tool designed to perform data migrations and transformations on your local machine. We value your privacy and aim for complete transparency in our operations.

2. Information We Collect

Bombardment does not collect any personal or usage data. All configurations, logs, and operations occur on your system. We do not track or send any data externally.

3. Data Processing

All processing and transformation of your data are performed locally within the tool on your machine. No external services or servers are involved unless you configure and invoke custom channels.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Bombardment does not share your data with any third parties. If you choose to integrate external endpoints (e.g., REST or gRPC targets), any data sent is directed by you to those endpoints, governed by their privacy policies.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have full control over every aspect of Bombardment:

  • Configuration: All settings are stored in files you manage.
  • Logs: Access or delete migration logs at any time.
  • Uninstallation: Remove the tool to eliminate all local configurations and data.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
